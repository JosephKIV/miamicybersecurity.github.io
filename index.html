--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} Port of the Django site's content/index.html. The old site grouped meeting posts into semesters with a custom template tag (`split_post_semesters`): month < 8 means Spring, otherwise Fall. There is no Liquid equivalent, so it is rebuilt in two passes below — first collect the distinct semester labels in post order (site.posts is already newest-first, so the labels come out newest-first too), then re-filter the posts for each label. Nothing in a post's front matter needs to say which semester it belongs to. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign semester_keys = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for post in site.posts -%} {%- assign mo = post.date | date: "%m" | times: 1 -%} {%- assign yr = post.date | date: "%Y" -%} {%- if mo < 8 -%}{%- assign season = "Spring" -%}{%- else -%}{%- assign season = "Fall" -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign key = season | append: " " | append: yr -%} {%- unless semester_keys contains key -%} {%- assign semester_keys = semester_keys | push: key -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- endfor -%}
